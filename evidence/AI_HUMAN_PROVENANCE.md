# AI–Human provenance

An AI system helped turn the supplied YAML portfolio into an executable compiler, validator, report generator, tests, CI, and deployment package. The underlying project specifications, selection rules, and portfolio thesis were supplied by the human operator.

This release does not prove unaided authorship. Human competence proof requires changing a contract, detecting deliberate cross-file drift, defending the two-source-of-truth decision, and validating the specifications against real repository paths.
